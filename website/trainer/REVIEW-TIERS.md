# Your review: profiles, the ECG course and cardiac arrest

Written 2026-10-25. Preview: https://claude.ai/artifact/AwDUiVLN2Lj9YToemaKGz1

## What's new in the trainer

- **Six profiles**: student, nurse, paramedic, resident, specialist (with a Cardiology or
  Emergency/ATI/internal/family medicine focus) and electrophysiologist. Each profile sees its own
  case list (core, stretch, skipped), its own question on every case (nurses and paramedics answer
  "what do you do first?" from four fixed actions), its own depth of explanation and its own drills.
  Change profile on the Profile page.
- **ECG course from zero**: 17 short lessons (6–15 min, 267 steps) in the Learn tab, from "how the
  heart writes the ECG" to a final systematic read and 10-second triage. Each profile has its own
  required, optional and skipped lessons; doctors skip the basics.
- **Cardiac arrest module**: 6 monitor strips (VF, fine VF, VT, asystole, PEA) from the MIT-BIH
  Malignant Ventricular Ectopy Database, plus a lesson, following ERC 2025.
- 168 profile questions across the 64 cases. Every text was checked against its tracing by a second
  reader. Your approved texts are unchanged.

## How to review

Open the review screen in the preview. Next to the case and diagnosis texts you already approved,
it now has four new groups: profile questions per case, plain labels and action cards, the
profiles, and the lessons. Approve or edit them as before.

For the questions below, answer by number (for example "A1 yes, B3 call the doctor"). Where you say
nothing, the default stays. **Section A is best given to an emergency physician from a UPU/SMURD
service** for one review session; it is about local scope of practice, not ECG reading.
