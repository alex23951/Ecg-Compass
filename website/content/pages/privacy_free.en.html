In short: we don't ask for personal data. There are no sign-up forms, no accounts and no tracking cookies. Your progress in the ECG trainer stays in your browser.

What we don't collect

You don't need to give us your name, email address or anything else to use the site and the ECG trainer. We use no advertising or tracking cookies and we sell no data to anyone.

Your progress in the trainer

Your answers, settings and review schedule are saved in your browser's local storage (localStorage), on the device you use. They never reach us. You can delete them at any time in your browser settings by clearing this site's data.

Hosting and statistics

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web server, Cloudflare technically receives your IP address and browser details on each visit, to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and shows us only totals (for example how many visits a page gets), without identifying you.

If you email us

If you write to {{ c.contact_email }} (for example to report an error), we use your address and message only to reply and to correct the content. We delete the message when it is no longer needed, at the latest after 12 months. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying and fixing errors (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

For the data in a message you send us, you have the right of access, correction, erasure, restriction and objection. Write to the address above. You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Romanian ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro) or the one where you live.
